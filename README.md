# Nelvra

**Distribution Profit System**

> Know where your stock is. Know where your money is. Know what to do next.

Nelvra is a business operating system for small and mid-sized local distributors. It is designed to improve the speed and profitability of the distribution money cycle rather than simply digitize administration.

## Current design partner

**MJS / Mitra Jaya Snack**

MJS is the first design partner and operational pilot. The product is being designed so MJS can validate the model without becoming a one-off custom application.

## Product model

BUY → PACK/PRODUCE → DISTRIBUTE → OUTLET → SELL-THROUGH → COLLECT → RESTOCK → REPEAT → PROFIT

Nelvra turns the operational events in this cycle into measurable decisions and next actions.

## Core promise

- Know where stock is.
- Know where cash is.
- Know which outlets and products deserve attention.
- Know what action should happen next.

## Product layers

1. **Distribution** — products, production/packing, inventory, field stock, outlets, visits, routes, returns.
2. **Profit** — COGS, contribution, collections, outstanding, stock velocity, outlet/product economics, route economics.
3. **Intelligence** — stockout risk, slow/dead stock, outlet priority, product priority, route priority.
4. **Action** — collect, restock, stop restock, move stock, revisit, reactivate, produce, buy.
