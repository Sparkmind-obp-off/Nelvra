# PHASE 6 — PROFIT INTELLIGENCE

## Objective
Transform operational records into an economic view of where value is created, trapped, or lost.

## Product economics
Calculate:
- unit COGS
- gross contribution/unit
- contribution by SKU
- movement/velocity
- slow and dead stock indicators.

## Outlet economics
Calculate:
- sell-through
- contribution
- collection behavior
- visit frequency
- outlet priority.

## Inventory intelligence
Classify:
- FAST
- NORMAL
- SLOW
- DEAD
- STOCKOUT RISK

Classification thresholds must be configurable and calibrated from real MJS behavior.

## Collection intelligence
Show:
- due today
- overdue
- outstanding balance
- age
- days-to-cash.

## Route economics
Show:
- route expense
- gross contribution
- route contribution
- contribution per visit where measurable.

## No false precision
Cost allocation must disclose the allocation method. The system must not imply a precise route or outlet profit figure when the underlying cost data cannot support it.

## Exit gate — INTELLIGENCE USEFUL
The owner can explain what sells, what makes money, where cash is stuck, where stock is stuck, and which outlets deserve attention.
