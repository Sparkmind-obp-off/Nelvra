# PHASE 10 — PRODUCTIZATION

## Objective
Convert the validated MJS implementation into a reusable product for other local distributors.

## Productization boundary
MJS-specific:
- customer data
- products
- outlets
- route geography
- pricing
- thresholds where customer-specific.

Reusable:
- core domain model
- inventory ledger
- collection model
- profitability engine
- action engine
- permissions
- audit architecture
- UX patterns.

## Multi-tenant readiness
- strict tenant isolation
- tenant settings
- configurable business rules
- tenant-scoped audit
- tenant-specific product/outlet data

## Onboarding
New customer should be able to configure:
- business profile
- product catalog
- outlet list
- pricing/margin
- visit cadence
- route cost method
- stock policies
- action thresholds.

## Packaging
Potential packaging dimensions:
- outlet count
- operational scale
- advanced intelligence
- integrations.

Do not finalize pricing until product usage and willingness-to-pay are validated.

## Commercial product promise
Primary:
> Move stock faster. Recover cash sooner. Move the profit.

Supporting:
> Know where your stock is. Know where your money is. Know what to do next.

## Expansion path
Potential future verticals:
- snacks
- FMCG
- beverages
- frozen food
- local wholesale/distribution.

## Second-customer test
The product passes productization when a second distributor can onboard with configuration rather than a code fork.

## Exit gate — PRODUCT READY
Nelvra has reusable architecture, repeatable onboarding, clear product boundaries, measurable value evidence, and a credible operating model for future customers.
