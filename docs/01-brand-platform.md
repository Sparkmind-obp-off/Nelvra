# Nelvra — Brand Platform

## 1. Brand

**NELVRA**

The name is intentionally abstract. It does not lock the company into snacks, inventory, logistics, finance, or AI.

## 2. Category

**Distribution Profit System**

This is the working product category, not merely a marketing label.

Nelvra is positioned between field distribution operations, inventory control, collection control, profitability analysis, and decision support.

## 3. Positioning

### One-line positioning

**Nelvra helps local distributors move stock faster, recover cash sooner, and operate around profitable actions.**

### Product explanation

Nelvra tracks the full distribution money cycle: what is packed, what leaves the base, what sits at each outlet, what sells, what is due to be collected, where route effort is being spent, and what the operator should do next.

## 4. Brand personality

- Calm
- Precise
- Practical
- Commercial
- Mature
- Quietly confident

Premium should come from **clarity and discipline**, not visual luxury.

## 5. What Nelvra is not

Nelvra is not positioned as:

- a generic ERP
- a POS replacement
- a delivery tracker
- a route-only optimizer
- an accounting suite
- an AI agency
- a generic CRM

AI can be added later, but the core product must create value without AI.

## 6. Tagline territory

Primary:

**Know the stock. Recover the cash. Move the profit.**

Alternative:

**Know where your stock is. Know where your money is. Know what to do next.**

## 7. Brand architecture

### Master

**NELVRA**

### Product

**NELVRA — Distribution Profit System**

### Pilot deployment

**NELVRA for MJS Snack**

### Future vertical extension

**NELVRA for Distribution**

The MJS relationship is a design-partner deployment, not a permanent limitation of the product.

## 8. Visual direction

### Wordmark

- Uppercase
- restrained geometric construction
- medium-to-semi-bold weight
- generous tracking
- no decorative script

### Symbol concept

A compact geometric mark representing **movement through states**:

stock → outlet → cash → profit

Avoid literal trucks, warehouses, barcodes, coins, or snack illustrations.

### UI personality

- neutral foundation
- high legibility
- strong numeric hierarchy
- sparse accent usage
- compact action controls
- clear operational status
- no visual noise

The interface should feel like a trusted field control surface rather than a marketing dashboard.

## 9. Brand test

A successful lock should look natural in all of these forms:

**NELVRA**

**NELVRA — Distribution Profit System**

**NELVRA for MJS Snack**

**NELVRA for Distribution**

If the name only works with one customer or one feature, it is not the final master brand.
